# Alibi Card

**Alibi Cards** allow an AppKeyId user to create a verifiable digital record of **where they were and when they were there**.

Like private Message Cards, Alibi Cards must be explicitly shared with a user before they can be acknowledged. When the user acknowledges an Alibi Card using their passkey, AppKeyId creates a permanent acknowledgement record containing the user's authenticated identity, the date and time of the acknowledgement, and, when **Precise Location** is enabled, the latitude and longitude reported by the user's device.

At its simplest, an Alibi Card allows a person to establish:

> **I was here, at this time, and I can prove that I acknowledged it.**

Unlike continuous location tracking, an Alibi Card records **specific moments of presence**. The user checks in by acknowledging the Card, creating an authenticated point in time and space. A series of acknowledgements can therefore create a chronological trail documenting a person's presence at different locations over time.

## Location-Constrained Alibis

Alibi Cards can also be **geofenced** using AppKeyId's **Constrain Location** feature. The author specifies a geographic location and an acceptable radius around it. The user must be within that area before AppKeyId will permit the Card to be acknowledged.

This can be useful when simply recording a location is not sufficient. The acknowledgement itself can be conditioned upon the user's device reporting that it is within the required geographic area.

An Alibi Card can therefore answer two different questions:

**Where were you when you acknowledged this?**

or

**Were you at the required location when you acknowledged this?**

Conversely, a location record may also provide supporting evidence that a person was somewhere other than a particular location at a particular time.

## Workplace and Professional Uses

Alibi Cards can provide a lightweight proof-of-presence system for organizations with employees and contractors working outside a traditional office.

Potential applications include:

- **Employee Presence** — Employees can establish that they were at a job site, office, customer location, or other workplace at a particular time.

- **Field Service** — Technicians, installers, inspectors, and repair personnel can establish when they arrived at a customer location.

- **Security Patrols** — Security personnel can acknowledge location-constrained Alibi Cards at checkpoints, creating a chronological record of their patrol.

- **Delivery and Pickup** — Drivers and couriers can establish their presence at pickup and delivery locations.

- **Construction and Inspections** — Contractors, engineers, and inspectors can create a record of their presence at a project site.

- **Event Attendance** — Staff, volunteers, contractors, and participants can establish their presence at an event or venue.

- **Remote and Traveling Employees** — Employees working away from a central office can periodically establish their location.

- **Proof of Visit** — Salespeople, caregivers, property managers, home-service providers, and other mobile professionals can document that they visited a particular location.

- **Chain of Custody** — Personnel handling equipment, documents, evidence, or valuable property can establish their presence at locations along a chain of custody.

## Supervised and Restricted-Movement Uses

Alibi Cards may also have applications when an individual is permitted to move freely but is expected to remain within—or stay away from—particular geographic areas.

Rather than continuously monitoring the individual, an Alibi Card can operate as an **authenticated check-in mechanism**. At designated times, the individual acknowledges the Card and creates a record of their identity, time, and reported location.

Potential applications include:

- **Parole and Probation** — A parolee or probationer required to remain within a particular geographic area could periodically acknowledge a location-constrained Alibi Card, creating a record that the required check-in occurred within the permitted area.

- **Curfews and Home Confinement** — A person required to be at home or another approved location during specified hours could perform periodic authenticated check-ins.

- **Work-Release Programs** — Participants could establish their presence at approved workplaces, residences, or other authorized locations.

- **Court-Ordered Geographic Restrictions** — Individuals subject to geographic restrictions could use Alibi Cards to document compliance with requirements concerning designated locations.

- **Registered Offender Supervision** — Where legally authorized, individuals subject to reporting or location requirements could periodically establish their presence at approved locations.

- **Restraining and Protective Orders** — An individual could voluntarily create a record demonstrating that they were at another location at a particular time, potentially providing supporting evidence of compliance with a geographic restriction.

This creates the possibility of **attested supervision**. Instead of continuously following someone's movements, AppKeyId creates authenticated checkpoints at particular times and locations.

## Family and Teen Check-Ins

The same capability can be useful in everyday family life.

Parents may want to know that their teenagers are where they said they would be without continuously tracking every movement they make. An Alibi Card provides a way for the teenager to **check in at meaningful moments**.

For example, a teenager attending a party could acknowledge an Alibi Card at an agreed-upon time. With **Precise Location** enabled, the acknowledgement creates a record showing when and where that check-in occurred.

Other examples include:

- **Parties and Social Events** — Check in from a party or friend's house.

- **Arriving Safely** — Confirm arrival at school, work, a hotel, or another destination.

- **Travel** — Periodically check in while traveling independently.

- **Curfews** — Establish arrival at home by an agreed-upon time.

- **After-School Activities** — Check in upon arriving at practice, work, tutoring, or another activity.

- **Emergency Situations** — Create an immediate time-and-location record showing where the family member was when they checked in.

For families, this offers a middle ground between no location information and continuous location sharing:

> **Let me know where you are when it matters.**

## Personal Alibis

Alibi Cards can also be used simply because a person wants an independent digital record of their whereabouts.

Someone entering a potentially contentious business meeting, exchanging property, visiting an unfamiliar location, meeting another person, or participating in an activity where their whereabouts might later become important could acknowledge an Alibi Card.

The objective is not necessarily to prove that something happened. It is to preserve a contemporaneous digital record that establishes **identity, time, location, and acknowledgement**.

Such a record may later provide supporting evidence that the person was at one location—and therefore potentially not at another—at a particular time.

## A Permanent Trail of Presence

The real value of Alibi Cards becomes apparent when individual acknowledgements are considered together.

A single acknowledgement establishes one point:

**Person → Time → Location**

Multiple acknowledgements can establish a chronological trail:

**Person → Location A → Location B → Location C → Time**

Unlike a conventional GPS application, time clock, or simple location check-in, an Alibi Card combines **authenticated identity, passkey acknowledgement, time, precise location, Card content, and a permanent acknowledgement record** as part of an Attested Communication.

This makes the concept useful across very different environments—from an employee checking into a construction site, to a teenager checking in from a party, to a supervised individual demonstrating compliance with a geographic restriction.

The underlying principle remains the same:

> **An Alibi Card creates an authenticated digital record of a person's presence at a particular place and time.**

Alibi Cards should not, however, be treated as infallible proof of physical presence. Location information supplied by consumer devices can vary in accuracy and may potentially be manipulated. For legal, employment, corrections, or law-enforcement applications, Alibi Card records should therefore be considered **supporting digital evidence**, with their use governed by applicable laws, court orders, employment policies, consent requirements, and organizational procedures.
