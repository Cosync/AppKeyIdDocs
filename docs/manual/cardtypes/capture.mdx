# Capture Card

**Capture Cards** solve a very common problem: you have a group of people and need to accurately collect their identity and contact information.

Traditionally, this means asking people to write down their name and email address, fill out a form, hand over a business card, or provide their information to someone who manually enters it into a database. Every one of these approaches introduces opportunities for error. Handwriting can be illegible, email addresses can be mistyped, information can be transcribed incorrectly, and people can deliberately provide false information.

Capture Cards approach the problem through **Attested Communication**.

Instead of asking someone to enter their identity into another form, AppKeyId uses the identity already associated with that person's AppKeyId account. The user authenticates and acknowledges the Capture Card using their passkey. This provides much stronger assurance that the person joining the group is the authenticated AppKeyId user associated with those credentials.

The result is essentially **authenticated contact capture**.

## How Capture Cards Work

Using a Capture Card begins with a **Team**. A Team is a collection of AppKeyId users that the Team owner wants to organize as a group.

The author first creates the Team into which new users will be captured and then creates a Capture Card associated with that Team.

Unlike a private Message Card, a Capture Card is **public**. The author does not need to know in advance who will respond to it. The Card can be distributed as a link, displayed as a QR code, printed on a sign, included in a presentation, or distributed through other media.

Anyone can encounter the Capture Card, but to join the Team they must be an AppKeyId user. When the user authenticates and acknowledges the Card, AppKeyId prompts them to join the associated Team.

Once they accept, their AppKeyId identity becomes part of the Team.

There is no handwritten signup sheet to decipher, no collection of business cards to enter later, and no need to manually transcribe email addresses from one system into another.

## Trade Show Lead Capture

A trade show provides a good example.

Instead of placing a clipboard on the booth counter and asking visitors to provide their names and email addresses, an exhibitor can display a large **AppKeyId Capture Card QR code**.

An interested visitor scans the QR code, signs into AppKeyId if necessary, acknowledges the Capture Card using their passkey, and joins the exhibitor's Team. The exhibitor now has the visitor's AppKeyId identity and contact information captured directly into a reusable group.

There can also be an incentive to participate. Visitors might be entered into a prize drawing at the end of the show or receive an immediate benefit—a cup of coffee, a donut, a promotional item, access to additional information, or some other reward.

The Capture Card effectively turns a QR code into an **authenticated lead-capture mechanism**.

## Parent-Teacher Associations

A Parent-Teacher Association could use the same mechanism to build a group of participating parents.

At the beginning of the school year, the PTA could distribute a Capture Card through a QR code at orientation, display it during a meeting, or include its link in school communications.

Parents acknowledge the Card and join the PTA Team. Instead of maintaining handwritten signup sheets and manually assembling mailing lists, the organization develops its membership list directly from participating AppKeyId users.

Once assembled, that Team can subsequently be used to distribute private AppKeyId Cards to the entire group.

## Other Uses for Capture Cards

The same model can be applied anywhere an organization needs to turn an unknown collection of people into an identifiable group.

- **Conferences and Events** — Capture attendees interested in a particular speaker, product, workshop, or subject. A QR code displayed at the end of a presentation can allow interested audience members to immediately join a follow-up Team.

- **Professional Associations and Clubs** — Enroll members at meetings, conferences, recruiting events, and other gatherings without manually maintaining signup sheets.

- **Volunteer Organizations** — Capture people willing to participate in a project, charity event, neighborhood cleanup, food drive, or other activity and immediately organize them into a Team.

- **Schools and Universities** — Create Teams for classes, clubs, alumni groups, parent organizations, study groups, and extracurricular activities.

- **Neighborhood and Homeowners Associations** — Allow residents to join neighborhood communication groups by scanning a QR code at a meeting or community event.

- **Churches and Community Organizations** — Build Teams for programs, committees, activities, volunteer opportunities, and other community groups.

- **Employee Onboarding and Training** — Capture employees attending an orientation or training session into a Team that can subsequently receive training materials and other Attested Communications.

- **Product Demonstrations** — Allow prospective customers to identify themselves as interested in a product and join a follow-up group immediately after seeing a demonstration.

- **Real Estate Open Houses** — Allow interested visitors to join a property-specific Team, providing the agent with a list of authenticated prospects without requiring a handwritten visitor log.

- **Emergency and Incident Response** — Assemble a Team of personnel arriving at an incident, staging area, shelter, or temporary work site.

## Exporting Captured Leads

The information collected through a Capture Card is not locked inside AppKeyId. The owner can **download an Excel-compatible spreadsheet containing the users captured into the Team**, together with the appropriate contact information associated with each lead.

The spreadsheet is structured for easy use with other business applications. Captured contacts can be imported into **CRM systems, email platforms, marketing databases, event-management systems, spreadsheets, and other third-party applications**.

For a trade show, for example, an exhibitor can display a Capture Card throughout the event, automatically collect interested attendees into a Team, and then download the resulting lead list for import into the company's existing sales or CRM system.

This eliminates much of the administrative work traditionally associated with lead capture. There are no handwritten forms to decipher, business cards to transcribe, or email addresses to manually re-enter.

The complete process becomes:

> **Scan → Authenticate → Acknowledge → Join Team → Export**

## From Capture to Communication

The real value of a Capture Card extends beyond collecting an email address.

Traditional lead-capture systems generally end with a row in a spreadsheet or CRM database. A Capture Card creates a relationship with an **authenticated AppKeyId user** and places that user directly into a Team.

That Team can then become the starting point for subsequent Attested Communication. Cards can be distributed to the group, members can acknowledge those communications, and the organization can maintain a continuing relationship with the people it originally captured.

Capture Cards therefore transform the traditional signup sheet into an **authenticated, automated, and immediately usable collection of people**.

What once required a clipboard, a stack of business cards, manual data entry, and subsequent cleanup can instead begin with something as simple as scanning a QR code.
