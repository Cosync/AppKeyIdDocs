# Manual

Welcome to the AppKeyId User Manual. This guide explains how to use AppKeyId to create, share, 
receive, and manage authenticated information using Attested Communication. 
Whether you are sending a private message to a colleague, publishing information through a 
QR code, or organizing users into teams, this manual will guide you through the features of 
the application.

The primary unit of information in AppKeyId is the Card. Cards are the basic objects used 
to share information between users. Throughout this manual, you will learn how to create 
different types of cards, distribute them to individual users or teams, and understand how 
recipients securely access and acknowledge them using FIDO2 passkeys.

You will also learn how to build and manage your Contacts. Contacts are trusted relationships 
between AppKeyId users that simplify sharing and collaboration. Once two users become contacts, 
they can easily exchange cards and participate together in teams.

For larger groups, AppKeyId provides Teams. Teams allow you to organize users into managed 
collections that can receive shared cards as a group. Teams can be populated manually by 
their managers or automatically through the use of Capture Cards, allowing users to securely 
join a team by following a link or scanning a QR code and authenticating with their passkey.

Finally, this manual describes the Profile menu, where you manage your personal account. 
Here you can update your profile information and avatar, register additional passkeys for 
your devices, configure application settings, manage your subscription plan, and review the 
activity logs that provide an auditable history of your AppKeyId activity.

The chapters that follow are organized around the major areas of the application—Cards, 
Contacts, Teams, and Profile Management—providing step-by-step guidance for using every 
feature of AppKeyId effectively and securely.

Throughout this manual, we use a compact web interface that mirrors the experience of using 
AppKeyId on a mobile device. AppKeyId is fully responsive and can be used interchangeably 
on both mobile devices and desktop computers. While the user experience is consistent 
across platforms, authoring and editing cards is generally more efficient on a desktop, 
where the larger screen and full keyboard provide a more comfortable workspace.
